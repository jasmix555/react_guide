**Swiper** is the go-to library for sliders and carousels. The things you see all the time on LPs — a hero slideshow, a carousel that scrolls products sideways, a testimonials slider — you can build quickly, with touch gestures and responsiveness included.

## What Swiper is good at {#why}

Building a slider yourself is quietly a lot of work. Finger movement while swiping, momentum, handling the edges, arrows, dots, autoplay, responsiveness — there is a lot to think about, and it is a part where bugs love to appear. **Swiper hands you all of this ready-made.**

- **Touch and drag** … swiping on phones and dragging on PCs both work from the start.
- **Responsive** … with `breakpoints` you can change the number of slides, like "3 slides from this width up."
- **Features as modules** … add only what you need: arrows (Navigation), dots (Pagination), autoplay (Autoplay), and more.
- **Accessibility** … the A11y module adds information for screen readers.

It is the first choice whenever you need "scroll sideways" or "a slideshow." Conversely, for a simple switch between 2 or 3 slides, you can build it yourself, like the [carousel recipe](/recipes/carousel). Choose based on the scale of what you are building.
